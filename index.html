01 / About

Engineer, but design-obsessed.

I live in the overlap between systems thinking and pixel-level craft.

Profile / 01

Born in Bagong Silang, Caloocan City, I started programming independently at age 13 driven by a deep curiosity for system architecture and mobile OS frameworks.

My early technical path led me to serve as an XDA Developers Forum Moderator (2012–2015) and mobile software consultant for Spex Appeal (2013–2015) and Power Mac Center (2013–2015). I engineered the MTA:SA multiplayer server IGN: International (2015–2018), identified game exploits in Stick Run, and worked alongside developers to patch vulnerabilities.

In 2017, I achieved a Global Top 26 ranking in Rules of Survival, representing the Philippines at the World Grand Championship in China, later producing featured content for NetEase's LifeAfter via facebook.com/callmerenzu.

After gaining hands-on field experience across Lalamove, Move It, JoyRide, and 4-wheel TNVS operations, I established ZE Documentation in June 2026 to help aspiring operators navigate regulatory requirements and join the ride-hailing industry.
